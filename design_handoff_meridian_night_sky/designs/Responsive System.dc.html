The Meridian · Responsive system

From a 320 px phone to a 3440 px ultrawide.

The shipped design covers 1280 to 2399 px, including the 2233 px monitor it was approved on. It stays exactly as it is. This board adds three tiers below it and two above it.

Frames are drawn at true pixel size, so every value can be measured. Gold numbers on a frame match the notes under it. The written spec is 07-responsive.md in the handoff folder.

01 · Hover is a bonus

Wrap every hover style in (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine). On touch, the first tap on a star, card or legend item selects it, showing what hover shows on desktop. Links inside the selection navigate.

02 · Touch targets

44 × 44 minimum under (pointer: coarse). Stars keep their drawn size but get a 44 px hit area; where hit areas overlap, the nearest star centre wins.

03 · Viewport units

Full-height sections use svh (min-height: max(100svh, 640px)); sheets and the menu use dvh. No bare 100vh anywhere.

04 · Safe areas

Add viewport-fit=cover. Header, menu, sheets and the dial pad with env(safe-area-inset-*), so nothing sits under a notch or the home bar.

05 · Short screens

(max-height: 760px), which catches 1280 × 720 and 1366 × 768 laptops: Home hero floor drops from 820 to 640, the aperture becomes min(400px, 34vw, 52svh), the Timeline dial 160 px.

06 · Landscape phones

(orientation: landscape) and (max-height: 500px): the header hides while a sky is in view, the Timeline shows the whole sky over a 120 px dial, and the Narrative goes back to two columns.

07 · Large screens in rem

Convert every type size, gutter, padding and max-width from px to rem once (px ÷ 16). XXL and 3XL then cost one line each: html { font-size: 112% } and 125%. Hairlines, stars and the dial stay in px.

08 · Motion and cost

The ceremony runs in short mode below 768 px or with Save-Data. Background stars: count = area ÷ 2800 (460 at 1440 × 900, about 120 on a phone), DPR capped at 2, paused offscreen. Reduced motion still wins.

09 · Images

srcset at 480, 800, 1200, 1800, 2600. sizes for cards: 50vw on XS, 33vw on S–M, 25vw on L, 320px above. Plates: min(100vw, 980px). Lazy-load everything below the first viewport.

10 · Keyboard

The "⌘K" label renders only under (pointer: fine). The shortcut itself, arrow keys on the dial and Esc on sheets work at every size, including an iPad with a keyboard.

01

Shell: header, menu and footer

One header component with three modes, chosen by width. The full-screen menu replaces both nav groups below 1024 px and is the only new surface on the site. The motto footer keeps its order at every size.

02

Home

The ceremony and the meridian line survive at every size. Below L, the flanks fold under the aperture, the sky chart changes its box but not its scale, and hover becomes tap.

03

Timeline

When the sky is narrower than 700 px it is drawn 2.6 times wider than the screen and slides past the meridian line, so the current year always sits on the line: risen objects to the left, the future to the right. The dial keeps its exact scale. Only the amount of it you can see changes.

04

Narrative

Below L, the pinned constellation becomes a sticky band above the story. The reveal logic does not change: the same observer, the same stars opening into circular images, the same segments drawing in.

05

Object

The plate goes edge to edge, the record folds away so the story starts sooner, and zooming moves into a full-screen viewer built for pinch and pan.

06

Collection, Narratives, The Island, Search

Index pages mostly reflow on their own once the grids and controls follow the token table. The two that need real changes are the Narratives band, which has to lose hover, and Search, which has to live above a keyboard.

07

Archive pages

All six share one change: the rail turns into a tab strip. Each page's own treatment then stacks label-over-text wherever it used a side column.

08

Large monitors, ultrawides and short laptops

Above 2400 px the design scales up as a whole rather than stretching. Diagrams are drawn to scale; the scale is in each caption.
