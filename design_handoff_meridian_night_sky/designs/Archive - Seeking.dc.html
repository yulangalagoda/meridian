Photographs of Ceylon, 1860 to 1910.

Albumen prints and albums by Joseph Lawton, W.L.H. Skeen & Co., Charles Scowen & Co., and the Colombo Apothecaries Co. Lawton’s views of Kandy and Anuradhapura are the standing priority.

Letters or documents of the British Governors of Ceylon, especially anything written from the island. Signed material of the independence generation: D.S.
